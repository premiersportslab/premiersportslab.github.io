Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from families considering Nona Basketball League.

What ages can participate?
We welcome players ages 3 to 14 across five divisions — Mini Hoops (3–5), D-League/Rookies (5–7), Sophomores (8–10), and All Stars (11–14). Players are grouped by age and skill level.
Do players need experience?
Not at all. The league caters to both beginners and intermediate players. Our coaches provide the right level of attention whether it's your player's first time on the court or they're ready to compete.
Where are practices and games held?
All sessions take place at SoFive (XL) Lake Nona, 12314 Suttner Ave, Orlando, FL 32827 — a premier indoor facility right in the heart of Lake Nona.
What should my player bring?
Comfortable athletic clothing, court shoes, and a water bottle. Basketballs and training equipment are provided during sessions.
How do I register?
Select the REGISTER TODAY button below, or reach out directly. We'll confirm your player's division and share payment details and the season schedule.
